# Research report

Follow the structure below. Take positions the evidence supports and consider the strongest alternative view. Interpret what the data means rather than listing it; use tables when they make a comparison clearer.

```markdown
# <a title of about six words with an active verb>
<BLUF paragraph: the key finding first, then significance and essential context. The reader can stop here.>

## <signpost header that states a specific insight>
<dense paragraphs with **bold key facts** and inline citations>
<!-- three to five signpost sections -->

## Open questions
<what could not be answered, why, and what would answer it>

## Conclusion
<one or two paragraphs of takeaways and implications; do not repeat earlier content>
```
