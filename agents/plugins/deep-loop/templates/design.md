```markdown
# <the design in one line>

## Requirements
<the requirements from the brief, each with how the design meets it>

## Current system
<the parts of the existing code the design touches, with absolute paths and line numbers>

## Design
<the recommended design and its key decisions, each with its rationale and evidence>

## Alternatives considered
<each rejected option and why it lost>

## Risks and critique
<the issues raised in critique, how each was resolved, and what remains open>

<decisions a human has to make, each with the options and what depends on it>
```
